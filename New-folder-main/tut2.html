Frequently Asked Questions

what is Homeez?

Homeez is a platform that connects homeowners directly to suppliers in their entire renovation process. Homeez uses the latest technology to offer an end-to-end digitalised solution - beginning from spatial planning using DIY tools like Design Now, all the way to managing the process using our unique Time Schedule tool.

what is the quatation Doctor

At 0% mark-ups, Homeez is committed to offer homeowners only the best and true prices available in the market so you can save time when comparing between quotations from various interior design firms. In the (unlikely) event that you manage to get a lower quote than us, send it to us and we’ll be able to match it or refund you within 7 days. Refer to our Terms and Conditions for more details!.

what is the lowest price

Quotation Doctor is created specifically to help homeowners review multiple quotations to narrow down to a quotation with the best prices available in the market. Once you upload the quotations, you can look forward to receiving news from Quotation Doctor within 5 days!
